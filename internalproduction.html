Before You Shoot
Studio Setup
Get this right once. Everything else follows.
Two Backdrop Setups
🌿 Essence Setup
Dark/Charcoal backdrop. The matte black box on dark background makes the flowers explode with colour. Dramatic. Premium. Powerful.

Key light 45° left, slightly above. Bounce card right. Vivid flower colours — don't wash them out.
🌸 With You Setup
Cream or White backdrop. The blush pink box needs softness behind it. Romantic and intimate.

Warm light — 3200K feel. Low and to the left. Golden hour warmth across the satin interior.
Lighting Rules
Product Shots
Key light overhead or 45°. Bounce card opposite side. No harsh shadows. Even, clean, luxury.
Face-Cam Shots
Key light 45° to face. Reflector other side — no raccoon eyes. Camera at eye level or slightly below. Never shoot looking down.
ASMR / Moody
Dim key light. Low and to the side. Long shadows between petals. More cinematic, less editorial.
Date Night Scene
Candle as primary light source. Studio light very dim or off. Let the flame flicker. Dark backdrop. Intimate.
Camera Positions
Overhead (flat lay): Camera directly above, parallel to surface. Carousel slides, product hero shots.

30° angle: Most versatile. Shows depth and dimension. Use for unboxing, reveals.

Macro/close-up: Fill the frame. Rose petals, necklace catch-light, chocolate detail.

Eye level: Face-cam authority posts. Never tilt down on yourself.
Golden Rules On Set
✦ Style before you shoot. Never adjust while recording.
✦ 3 takes minimum on every scene. You need options.
✦ 4K if possible — you'll crop for both IG (4:5) and TikTok (9:16).
✦ ASMR = silence. Close windows, turn off fans, before those shots.
✦ The sealed box first. Always shoot closed before opened.
✦ Natural reactions only. Don't direct expressions — capture them.

02
ASMR Unboxing — Essence Gift Box
Essence IG + TikTok
Goes live: Apr 13 · Reel · 30–45 sec
★ Priority
Concept
The two-stage reveal in real time. Tissue paper first — then the flowers. No jump cuts. Every sound matters. This is the product experience as content. Your helper does the unboxing. You operate camera.
Setup
Backdrop: Dark charcoal · Light: Dim key, low and to the side — moody but detailed · Camera: 20–30° angle to see box depth · Sound: Quiet the room. Close windows. External mic if available pointed at box.
Shot Sequence — Real Time No Cuts
①
Closed Box
[0:00–0:04]
Static shot. Matte black box. Logo visible. Total silence for 2 seconds. Then hands enter from bottom of frame.
"Close your eyes. Open this."
②
The Lid Lift
[0:04–0:08]
Both hands on sides of lid. Slow, deliberate lift. The hinge sound. Pause at top. Let tissue paper sit for 2 full seconds before anything else.
③
The Tissue Reveal
[0:08–0:14]
One hand lifts ESSENCE card slowly. Sets it aside. Then both hands gently part tissue from center outward. The rustle is the sound moment. Flowers appear from beneath. This is your peak.
④
The Flower Hold
[0:14–0:20]
Don't touch anything. Camera slowly pushes in toward the flowers over 4–6 seconds. Let the viewer absorb the colour.
⑤
The Jewellery Box
[0:20–0:26]
One hand reaches in, opens jewellery box. The hinge click. Pause on necklace and earrings 3 seconds. Tilt box so moissanite catches light.
⑥
The Chocolate
[0:26–0:32]
Hand picks up one artisan chocolate gently between two fingers. Holds it near a rose. Just holds it — doesn't eat it.
"The Essence Gift Box."
⑦
Full Pull-Back
[0:32–0:40]
Camera pulls back slowly to show complete open box. Everything in frame. Hold 4 seconds. Then slowly lower lid. That final thud.

3 Moments a Thoughtful Gift Changes Everything
With You IG + TikTok
Goes live: Apr 8 · Reel · 20–30 sec · FIRST POST LIVE
★ Priority
Concept
Three back-to-back micro-stories. Each 7 seconds. Fast cuts between them. WITH YOU box is the thread. No words needed — the gesture is everything. Your helper is the receiver in scenes 1 and 2. Both of you in scene 3.
Setup
Backdrop: Cream/white · Light: Warm key light · Outfit: Smart-casual for all three scenes — keeps it cohesive in edit
3 Scenes
1
The Apology
You sit across from your helper at studio table. You slide the closed blush WITH YOU box slowly across the table toward them. No eye contact at first — then they look up at you. No words. The gesture is everything. 3 takes minimum.
"When sorry isn't enough."
2
The Proud Moment
Helper holds a piece of paper (printed document — graduation, promotion, anything). You enter frame and place the pink box next to the paper. They look at the box, then at you. A real smile — don't direct it, capture it.
"When you need them to know you see them."
3
The Surprise
Helper sits, eyes closed or looking away. You place the WITH YOU box in front of them. They open their eyes. Genuine reaction. Don't direct it — just capture it. This is the most important shot of the three.
"When words come second."
End Card Shot Needed
Sealed pink box, gold seal centered. Static. 3 seconds. This is your clean close.
