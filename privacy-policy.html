Privacy Policy

Curator Atelier respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

When you submit your information through our website or advertising forms, we may collect details such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Address or city (when provided)
  • Any details you include in your message or request

We collect this information solely to respond to inquiries, confirm service availability, provide requested services, and communicate with you regarding your request.

We do not sell or rent the personal information you submit through our forms. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and receive your message (for example, our form and email providers), and only so we can respond to you.

Analytics and advertising

This website uses cookies and similar technologies from Google, Meta, and LinkedIn:

  • Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed and whether an inquiry is sent. We have turned off Google signals and ads personalization for Google Analytics, and we do not use Google Analytics data for advertising. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
  • Google Ads tag and Meta Pixel help us measure our advertising and may be used to show our ads to people who have visited this site (remarketing) on Google and on Meta's platforms (Facebook and Instagram). These tools record things like the pages you visit and whether you send an inquiry, along with information from your browser and device. They do not receive the contents of the forms you submit.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag helps us understand visits from LinkedIn and measure LinkedIn advertising. It records pages you visit and basic browser/device information. It does not receive the contents of the forms you submit.

Google, Meta, and LinkedIn process this information under their own privacy policies: How Google uses information from sites that use its services, Meta Privacy Policy, and LinkedIn Privacy Policy.

How to opt out

  • Google Analytics: install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google ads: turn off personalized ads in My Ad Center.
  • Meta ads: adjust your Meta ad preferences, including activity from other websites.
  • LinkedIn: adjust your LinkedIn ad settings.
  • Many advertising companies: use YourAdChoices or NAI opt-out.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work.

Your information is stored securely and used only as necessary to deliver our services or communicate with you regarding your request.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at hello@curatoratelier.com.
