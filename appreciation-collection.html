Questions

Before you inquire.

Is there a minimum order?

Proposals are shaped around your recipient list, from a handful of key relationships to company-wide programs. Share an approximate quantity in your inquiry and we will advise on the right approach.

Can gifts carry our brand?

Yes. Presentation and correspondence can carry your brand where it suits the relationship, always quietly and in keeping with the gift.

Do you deliver to individual recipients?

Yes. Gifts can be delivered to individual addresses across the United States, or to one location for you to present in person. Delivery is coordinated around the date you choose.

How is pricing determined?

Each program is proposed individually. Recipients, quantities, service depth, personalization and delivery all shape the proposal, so we begin with your needs rather than a fixed price list.

Is it only for the holidays?

No. The Appreciation Collection is our year-round gifting platform for client closings, onboarding, milestones, resident appreciation and moments of thanks throughout the year.

When should we start planning for the holidays?

We recommend beginning holiday planning early. Production and installation schedules vary by scope, quantities, location and availability. Timelines are confirmed with each proposal.

Can I buy a single gift?

Individual gifts are available from our shop at curatorateliergifts.com.
